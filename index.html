RESEARCH WORKFLOW

Start with the texts, not a claim.

The workflow audits file encoding and boundaries, preserves source offsets, measures collocates in sentence-bounded ±5-token windows, and evaluates candidate-bearing sentence embeddings against length-matched non-candidate controls. Candidate lists are operational definitions, not exact translation equivalents.

The three texts share a five-part structure, but they are not aligned sentence by sentence. Results describe this novel, not translation in general.

Similarity needs context.

A SECOND LENS

Choose a language pair.

Candidate and control centroids are normalized multilingual MiniLM embeddings. The delta is modest, and the interval describes sampling variation within this corpus.

Bars begin at cosine 0.90 for readability; full values are printed. These are corpus-level centroids, not sentence alignments or translation-quality scores.

INTERPRETATION AND LIMITS

Measured variation, modest claims.

The results show language-specific lexical associations and a small baseline-adjusted concentration of red-related contexts. They do not establish directional cultural semantic shift. Stronger claims require aligned passages, human annotation and sensitivity checks on candidate sets.

Copyrighted novels, sentence excerpts and text-recoverable embeddings are not distributed here.
